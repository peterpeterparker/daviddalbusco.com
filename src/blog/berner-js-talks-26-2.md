I'm demoing the recent projects I built at [BärnerJS Talks #26-2](https://www.meetup.com/barner-js-talks/events/313743608/).

One of them is a [GitHub Action](https://github.com/peterpeterparker/blog-to-newsletter-action) that reads a new blog post, generates a newsletter draft using LLM (Claude API), and delivers it to subscribers (using Mailchimp) after an approval tap (in Telegram).

This very post is the source I'll use to trigger that pipeline during the demo.
