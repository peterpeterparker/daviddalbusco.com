Oh Graubünden, autumn colors suit you so well.

I followed the [same trail](/trails/2023/chur-dreibundenstein-chur) as two years ago, but in the opposite direction, and going up this way felt a bit easier.

There are ways to shorten it, probably saving 2 to 3 km or even more, and you can also head back to Chur by cable car.
