This trail follows the ascent part of the Swissalpine K42. A bit technical in my experience on the way down, but absolutely quiet and lovely in nice weather.

Useful links:

- [Swissalpine](https://swissalpine.ch)
- [Lift status in the area](https://live.laax.com/en/lifts)

Notes:

The way down by cable car is (too) expensive, 30 CHF. No discount for Halbtax holders. Also, at the time I ran the trail, the lift was only open on weekends.
