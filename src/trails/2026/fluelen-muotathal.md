What I like about Uri is that they say "salut" in plain French as a greeting 😄. The view was really great too. I last followed this itinerary in summer, the autumn colors suited it really well. Plus, the temperature was perfect for running!

Side note: I messed up the GPX track. I tried following a path that I assumed would join the route, but it didn't, and I also missed a few turns. Long story short, this trail is longer than it should be. You can grab my previous effort [here](/trails/2024/fluelen-muotathal).
